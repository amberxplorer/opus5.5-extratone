      FUSION POINT · where rhythm turns into pitch

      ⚠ Before you start

      Flashing lights. This piece has bright colours, fast motion and occasional full-screen flashes. Kicks are never drawn as blinking lights, and flashes are limited to well under three per second, but it may still be uncomfortable for people who are sensitive to flicker and could trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If that could be you or anyone watching, turn on reduced flashing below or don't continue.

      Harsh, loud audio. Extratone is kick drums at over 1000 BPM: distorted, buzzing, relentless. It is synthesized live in your browser. Start with your volume low.

      Your system is set to reduce motion, so reduced flashing starts switched on.

      About three minutes, then it re-seeds and plays a new variation. 180 BPM gabber → accelerando → fusion → harmonic-series tempos → Rhythmicon → Risset rhythm → Kontakte → overflow. Every sound is synthesized with the Web Audio API and every picture is drawn on a canvas: no samples, no libraries.
